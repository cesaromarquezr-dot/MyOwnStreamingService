# Collections and Catalog Examples Fix

## Why the home screen and Watch > Collections disagreed

The Home `Collections For You` rail could construct an on-the-fly `Action Movies Collection` from title keywords. That virtual collection was not persisted in `AppController.collections`, which is what Watch > Collections browses. The Action Movies theme is now generated by the same shared automatic-collection index as other automatic groups, so the home rail and Watch > Collections resolve the same collection. The theme is restricted to movie titles, not TV shows or audio.

## Local catalog examples

The local browse catalog now seeds clearly labeled, non-playable metadata examples for:

- 33 Marvel Cinematic Universe movie titles and `The Falcon and the Winter Soldier`.
- `Spider-Noir`, explicitly separated from the MCU continuity.
- `How to Train Your Dragon`, all eight Harry Potter films, `The Incredibles`, and `Finding Nemo`.
- `Wizards of Waverly Place`, `That's So Random!`, `The Suite Life of Zack & Cody`, and `American Dragon: Jake Long`.
- Album examples for `1989` (Taylor Swift), `Rare` (Selena Gomez), `YHLQMDLG` (Bad Bunny), and `Colores` (J Balvin), with sample track entries to make artist pages navigable.

Movie and TV examples carry starter cast/crew metadata for browsing actor profiles and filmographies. Album examples carry performer metadata for artist/album details. These are catalog examples only: they do not contain video/audio files or promise playback. Starter credits are not exhaustive; a configured TMDB key can enrich supported movie/TV details with provider-sourced data.

## Where to find them

- Open Movies for the movie examples.
- Open TV Shows for the show examples.
- Open Music and use Artists or Albums for the four album/artist examples.
- Open a movie/show's details and tap a cast/crew name for that person's catalog profile.
- Open an album's details and tap its artist credit, or use Music > Artists for the music-focused artist profile.
- Open Watch > Collections to find the shared `Action Movies Collection` and automatic franchise/saga collections.

The examples are added to the local catalog and sanitized offline metadata cache; they are not uploaded as owned media files to the server.
