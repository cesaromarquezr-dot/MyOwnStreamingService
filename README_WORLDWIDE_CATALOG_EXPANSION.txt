WORLDWIDE CATALOG TEST EXPANSION

Expanded lib/international_tmdb_test_section.dart with additional movie and TV lookup examples from more countries and language markets, including Colombia, Indonesia, the Philippines, Iran, Egypt, Nigeria, South Africa, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Italy, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Poland, and others.

The fixtures are test-only. Running the section performs live TMDB metadata lookups when the TMDB service is configured. It does not add these entries to the user's personal library and does not include video streams or playable media.

Review the sample titles and language labels against your preferred TMDB locale before using this as a production catalog. Some titles are co-productions or multilingual works, so country/language labels are broad test labels rather than authoritative distribution metadata.
